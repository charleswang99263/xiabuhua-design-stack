# Direction vocabulary and profile router

This is a working design model, not a claim that design history consists of 27 mutually exclusive styles. The four supplied sheets illustrate 20 terms; they are not verified websites or motion evidence. Contemporary labels have uneven definitions. Linked profiles separate documented concepts, observed examples and our original web implementation interpretation. Research checked 2026-09-07; verify live sources again for current product behavior or asset use.

## Build a complete direction across layers

1. **Task structure**: what people read, compare or operate. This constrains semantics and density, not the aesthetic answer.
2. **Visual language**: dominant relationships among type, composition, imagery, color and form.
3. **Supporting composition/material**: grouping, layering, texture and physical metaphor.
4. **Temporal/spatial strategy**: how objects, views and information change through time or space.

Use only the layers the brief needs. Do not make the user fill a four-part menu or require style mixing. Roles overlap: collage can organize an entire identity; material can lead a complete direction. The test is a coherent executable thesis, not its category label.

## Task structures, not competing visual styles

| Entry | Preserve while styling | Code-level implications |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Functional / operational / 功能运营 | Task frequency, comparison, actions and recovery | Semantic forms/tables; explicit focus/selected/error states; usable density. No mandatory blue-white shell. |
| Document / knowledge / 知识文档 | Reading sequence, outline, references and retrieval | Semantic headings/articles; language-specific reading measure; anchors, footnotes and code. No mandatory paper or serif. |
| Data narrative / analytical / 数据分析 | Quantities, encodings, uncertainty and evidence | Accessible figures/tables; shared scales and denominators; annotation hierarchy. Styling must not change quantities or hide caveats. |

These three route to existing report/web-app methods. The [W3C table tutorial](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/tables/) supports semantic header/data relationships, not a universal visual theme. [IBM editorial rhythm guidance](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/layout/tips-and-techniques/) demonstrates content-driven repetition and variation, not the definition of all editorial design.

## Resolve and compare

Read relevant profiles, then [translate to implementation](style-implementation.md). Show how relationships affect an expressive area, a dense/ordinary area and a control/state. Do not select three entries just because they occupy different rows. Compare completed directions, not labels. Keep requirements, content and effort comparable; do not force arbitrary novelty or silently exclude imagery-led directions for coding convenience.

The [reference branch](../../brand-style-reference/SKILL.md) also contains brand studies and migrated presentation examples, with no preference by output format. Upstream values describe that example and never override user selection.
